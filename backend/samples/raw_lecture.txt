Process Scheduling

# What is scheduling
The scheduler decides which process runs next and for how long.
This affects throughput, waiting time and response time.

Preemptive: the scheduler can interrupt a running process.

## Common algorithms
1. First Come First Served
2. Shortest Job First
3. Round Robin
   - each process gets a fixed time slice
   - simple and fair
4. Priority Scheduling

Key metrics:
turnaround = completion - arrival

Warning: priority scheduling can cause starvation.

e.g. a low priority job may wait forever under constant high priority load

> Scheduling is the art of deciding who waits.
